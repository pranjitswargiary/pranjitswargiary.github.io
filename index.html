A little about

I believe leadership is not simply a position — it is the responsibility to listen, serve and help create positive change.

I am an undergraduate student at Rangapara College (Autonomous), affiliated with Gauhati University. My interests include student welfare, youth engagement, community development, environmental conservation and the preservation of Bodo culture and heritage.

Through volunteering and campus activities, I aim to develop practical leadership skills, work with diverse groups and contribute to meaningful initiatives in my community.
